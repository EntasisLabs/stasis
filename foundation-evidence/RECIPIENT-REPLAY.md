## Implemented contract

1. **Real signal dispatcher:** each logical ID represented in captured waits is sampled once. Every wait for that ID uses that same incarnation sample, including a missing/mismatched sample. The subsequent direct-key read cannot add a replacement for an already-observed ID. Direct-key buffering for an otherwise unknown current job remains supported. Custom correlation dispatch and existing unbound buffering remain supported. This is per-logical-recipient coherent fail-closed capture, not a global multi-job transaction. Insertion, wait completion and wakeup remain separate calls.

2. **Terminal obligations:** native Job/receipt `terminal_cycle` is initialized to 1 on insertion/child materialization. Native DeadLetter-to-Enqueued replay CAS advances it atomically; ordinary acquisition, Running, retry and deferral preserve it. Edges capture `parent_terminal_cycle` and a canonical `parent_version_floor`. The floor is a lower bound, not epoch equality. Application filtering AND native memory/Surreal settlement enforce the persisted binding against the exact current terminal row or retained genuine receipt. Old A failure/control outcomes cannot discard B edges, and B success cannot settle old A producer obligations. Worker staging remains grant-bound; Surreal publication revalidates persisted staged bindings. Native publication preserves canonical cycle authority even if the worker edits its local snapshot. New child materialization issues its own cycle.

3. **Migration:** missing parent/edge cycle is unknown (zero), missing edge floor is None. Ambiguous pending obligations fail without lifecycle acknowledgement; unknown parent cycles are not adopted by producer registration or replay. Mixed old/new writers are unsupported. Quiesce and explicitly reconcile only provable native history or quarantine unresolved records under existing operator policy. No automatic historical backfill/migration, fabricated receipt or recovery redo is claimed. Hand-built Job/continuation literals require the new fields. See `docs/native-execution-ownership.md`.

An intermediate floor-only design was deliberately superseded after following reverse-order delivery: it could let B success settle an old pre-A producer edge. `corrected-probes.log`, `supplement-native-rerun.log` and `final-native.log` precede the complete cycle design and are NOT the final candidate's evidence. `supplement-native.log` is a retained fixture-name compilation failure, not a product safety assertion. `cycle-native-initial.log`/`cycle-native-final.log` are intermediate focused passes. Final passing receipts are the explicitly named final logs below. No earlier failures or intermediate runs are counted as final success.
